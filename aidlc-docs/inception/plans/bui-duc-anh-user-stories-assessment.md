## Decision

Create stories and personas that describe discovery, scientific understanding, academic progress, certificate review, community contribution, accessible navigation and safe publication. Keep all stories independently understandable and traceable to requirements; avoid fictional visitor statistics, implementation choices or delivery schedules.
